Rather than a formal lecture, the first session of Spring 2026 was a kick-off discussion: an open, informal space to reflect on what the group has explored so far, to discuss topics and formats of interest for the semester, and to collectively shape a light-touch plan for upcoming sessions.

The new format places greater emphasis on student-initiated participation, low-pressure and flexible forms of engagement, and knowledge exchange that fits naturally alongside PhD research. No preparation was required - listening in, sharing thoughts, or simply exploring the group were all equally welcome.
