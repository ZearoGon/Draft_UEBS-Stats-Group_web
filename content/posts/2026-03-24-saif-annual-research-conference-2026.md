The 2026 SAIF Annual Research Conference, organised by the Shanghai Advanced Institute of Finance (SAIF) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, provides a platform where high-quality and innovative research in financial economics is presented and discussed in an engaged and interactive environment. 14-15 May 2026, Shanghai, China. Registration deadline: 26 April 2026.

Dr Zexun Chen's note: "Although you may not submit a paper to the conference, it is still a good opportunity to attend if you're in China during the conference period. This conference is free for attendance."
