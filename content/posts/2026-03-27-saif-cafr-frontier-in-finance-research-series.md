The first workshop under the SAIF-CAFR Frontier in Finance Research Series, jointly organised by the Shanghai Advanced Institute of Finance (SAIF) and the China Academy of Financial Research (CAFR) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 17-18 May 2026 in a combined online-offline format. Designed for young researchers, the series presents distinguished scholars who introduce participants to the frontiers of academic research in finance and to cutting-edge research methodologies.

Speakers: Bo Bian (Assistant Professor of Finance, UBC Sauder - empirical corporate finance, regulation and the digital economy), Winston Wei Dou (Associate Professor of Finance, Wharton - asset pricing and capital markets) and Song Ma (Professor of Finance and Entrepreneurship, Yale SOM - innovation economics, AI and big data). Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen.
