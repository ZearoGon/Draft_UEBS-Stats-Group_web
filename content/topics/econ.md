<h2>Key Concepts & Frameworks</h2>
  <p>We summarize the important econometric concepts, frameworks, tools and methodologies below. The econometrics section covers a range of fundamental techniques used in empirical research across business disciplines.</p>

  <h3>Core Areas</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)</strong> - The foundational regression technique for estimating linear relationships</li>
    <li><strong>Instrumental Variables (IV)</strong> - Addressing endogeneity through external instruments</li>
    <li><strong>Panel Data Methods</strong> - Fixed and random effects for longitudinal data analysis</li>
    <li><strong>Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE)</strong> - Parametric estimation under distributional assumptions</li>
    <li><strong>Generalized Method of Moments (GMM)</strong> - Flexible estimation using moment conditions</li>
    <li><strong>Time Series Analysis</strong> - ARIMA, VAR, and cointegration methods</li>
  </ul>
