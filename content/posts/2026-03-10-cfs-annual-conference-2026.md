The 2026 Centre for Statistics Annual Conference brings together researchers in data science and statistics from across the University of Edinburgh, Heriot-Watt University and associated institutions. Friday 19 June 2026, 9:00-16:30, Larch Lecture Theatre, Nucleus Building, King's Buildings campus. The programme includes six invited speakers from Schools and Centres across the University, snap talks from early-career researchers in the School of Mathematics, and a lunchtime poster session open to all - anyone interested in presenting a poster could note it on the registration form.

Organised by Sara Wade, Tim Cannings and Jordan Richards. Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen.
