Mechanism analysis is a critical component of empirical research and effective academic storytelling. Once the baseline relationship and causal effect between X and Y are established, the next natural question is: why does X cause Y? Through which mechanisms or channels does this effect operate? This presentation introduces the concept of mechanism analysis: what it is, why it matters, and how to conduct it. It reviews various methodological approaches to mechanism analysis and illustrates them with examples from publications in top-tier journals.
