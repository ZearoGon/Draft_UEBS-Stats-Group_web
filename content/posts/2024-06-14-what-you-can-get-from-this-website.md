The resources from this website are mainly for researchers in the area of finance. Finance research, when combined with data, is closely related to statistics - especially in empirical work. As researchers working on different topics in finance, we find it useful to record the statistical tools we come across, including models and tests, and this website records those tools as blogs.

Each topic corresponds to an area one of the maintainers focuses on: credit research, risk management and asset pricing. Under each topic, statistical tools that are usually used for research in that area are made available. The schedule announces our discussion sessions, which share the knowledge we blog about in a seminar-presentation mode. To join a discussion session, use the subscription form on the home page.

Though we primarily do research in finance, we believe this statistical knowledge is also meaningful to researchers in accounting, marketing, operations research and other business-school fields. If you would like to contribute to the materials or the maintenance of the website, you are more than welcome to contact us.
