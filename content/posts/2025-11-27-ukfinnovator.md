UKFinnovator is a regional student innovation competition powered by the UKFin+ Network: a two-day hackathon in which multi-disciplinary students from regional universities collaborate to solve real-world challenges set by industry, regulators and charities. It focuses on creative skills and resilience as much as coding, combining technical expertise with human skills to imagine bold new solutions for financial technology. Challenges were set by companies including Allsee Technologies, Unique Tech Solutions, GFA Exchange, Bettr Social, Solana Superteam UK and NoCodeLab.ai.

Members of the group took part in the February 2026 edition in Birmingham - see the photo gallery at the top of the page.
