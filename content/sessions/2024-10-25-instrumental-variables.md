This section introduces the concept of Instrumental Variables (IV) in econometric analysis, particularly focusing on addressing endogeneity issues such as omitted variable bias, reverse causality and measurement errors. It highlights the key conditions for a valid instrument, including exogeneity and relevance, and provides real-world examples to demonstrate IV application. The section also includes statistical outputs, tests for instrument identification, and offers resources for finding potential instruments in empirical research.
