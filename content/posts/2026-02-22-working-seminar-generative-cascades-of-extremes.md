A Centre for Investing Innovation working seminar by Professor Miguel de Carvalho (School of Mathematics): *Generative Cascades of Multivariate Extremes*. The talk motivates the notion of cascades of extremes - as recently introduced by de Carvalho, Ferrer & Vallejos (2026, *Extremes*) - such as a tsunami followed by an earthquake or a cascade of stock market losses, and argues that the sequential nature of cascading extremes makes them particularly appealing for generative modelling.

Tuesday 10 March 2026, 10:00-11:30, Room 3.43, Edinburgh Futures Institute. Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen.
