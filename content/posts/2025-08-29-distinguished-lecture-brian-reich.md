A free public lecture by Professor Brian Reich, Gertrude M. Cox Distinguished Professor of Statistics at NC State University and an expert in spatial statistics, Bayesian hierarchical modelling and environmental sciences.

*Leveraging deep learning for spatial statistics problems.* Fitting Gaussian Processes provides interpretable aleatoric uncertainty quantification for spatio-temporal fields, but suffers from scalability issues; spatio-temporal deep learning models scale but typically assume a simplistic independent covariance structure. The lecture discussed how the two can be combined.

Friday 5 September 2025, 15:30, Edinburgh Futures Institute (Room 2.35). Organised by Professor Miguel de Carvalho, partially funded by Prob_AI and jointly organised with GAIL and GLE²N. Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen ("Distinguished person in Bayesian stats").
