<h2>Key Concepts & Methodologies</h2>
  <p>Asset pricing research draws on a rich set of statistical and econometric tools to examine whether expected returns vary across assets and over time. Below are the core frameworks commonly used in the field.</p>

  <h3>Core Areas</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>CAPM & Factor Models</strong> - Capital Asset Pricing Model and multi-factor extensions (Fama-French, Carhart)</li>
    <li><strong>Fama-MacBeth Regression</strong> - Two-pass procedure for testing cross-sectional return predictability</li>
    <li><strong>GMM-based Tests</strong> - Hansen-Jagannathan bounds and stochastic discount factor tests</li>
    <li><strong>Anomalies & Trading Strategies</strong> - Momentum, value, size effects and their statistical significance</li>
    <li><strong>High-Frequency Econometrics</strong> - Realized volatility, microstructure noise, and intraday patterns</li>
    <li><strong>Machine Learning in Asset Pricing</strong> - Prediction with high-dimensional covariates</li>
  </ul>
