The Centre for Statistics invited academics and researchers to a half-day research afternoon on copula theory and its applications, with a particular focus on finance, insurance and environmental sciences. The programme featured three to four research talks showcasing applied case studies from the University of Edinburgh, Edinburgh Napier University and Heriot-Watt University, followed by informal discussion and networking towards future collaboration.

Wednesday 6 May 2026, 14:00-17:00, JCMB 6206, University of Edinburgh. Attendance was free with registration. Organised by Ozan Evkaya and Merve Coskun with the support of the Centre for Statistics; forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen.
