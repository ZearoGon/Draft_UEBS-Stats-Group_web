The first seminar of the 2025/26 academic year. Data with an unknown distribution can often be characterised by a small set of low-dimensional factors; PCA and auto-encoders are popular for that reduction, but a lower-dimensional representation alone is not enough when we also want to generate data that looks as true as possible - which is where the variational auto-encoder comes in. The topic was revisited in a second session on 31 October 2025.
