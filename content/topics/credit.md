<h2>Key Concepts & Methodologies</h2>
  <p>Credit research employs a wide range of statistical methods to model and predict credit events. The field continues to evolve with the integration of machine learning techniques alongside traditional econometric methods.</p>

  <h3>Core Areas</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Credit Scoring Models</strong> - Logistic regression, discriminant analysis, and scorecard development</li>
    <li><strong>Default Prediction</strong> - Structural models (Merton), reduced-form models, and hazard rate estimation</li>
    <li><strong>Portfolio Credit Risk</strong> - Copula models, CreditMetrics, and loss distribution estimation</li>
    <li><strong>Machine Learning for Credit</strong> - Random forests, gradient boosting, neural networks for credit assessment</li>
    <li><strong>Stress Testing</strong> - Scenario analysis and macroeconomic factor models</li>
    <li><strong>Credit Derivatives</strong> - CDS pricing and credit spread modelling</li>
  </ul>
