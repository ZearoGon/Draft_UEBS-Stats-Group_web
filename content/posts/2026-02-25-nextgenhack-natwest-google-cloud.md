ECFI (the Edinburgh Centre for Financial Innovations) led a student hackathon on Saturday 21 March 2026 in collaboration with UEBS Events, Google, NatWest and Edinburgh Venture Point, a student initiative. It offered a one-day opportunity to use Google Cloud facilities for free and to work on real-world challenges with NatWest.

Dr Zexun Chen attended the information session and recommended it to the group: "it looks quite engaging - if you have the time and interest, I would highly recommend participating."
