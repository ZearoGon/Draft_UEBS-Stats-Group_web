The Knowledge Transfer Partnership (KTP) between the University of Edinburgh Business School and Kodamai recruited a Data Scientist (KTP Associate). The 27-month position focuses on predictive modelling, causal inference, probabilistic forecasting and decision intelligence for next-generation agentic AI systems in financial services, working closely with both academia and industry, developing production-grade AI systems, publishing research and gaining commercial experience.

Primarily based at Kodamai's offices in central Glasgow with regular visits to the University of Edinburgh. Salary up to £60,000 including market supplement. Application deadline: 20 July 2026, 23:59.

A second KTP Associate - an Agentic AI Security and Privacy Research Engineer - was recruited by the School of Informatics as part of the same Innovate UK project; the two Associates work together on a secure and trustworthy agentic AI platform. Shared by Dr Zexun Chen, who leads the partnership on the Business School side.
