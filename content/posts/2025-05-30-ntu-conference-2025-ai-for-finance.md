The inaugural NTU Conference 2025 on AI for Finance took place in person on 7 July 2025 at the Gaia Auditorium, Nanyang Business School, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, as part of the NTU AI & Business Forum Series. It brought together academics, researchers and industry practitioners to explore how AI is transforming finance - through keynote speeches, research presentations, poster sessions and panel discussions on topics such as algorithmic and high-frequency trading strategies.

Participation was free for students; a registration fee of S$200 applied to non-students. Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen via the UEBS ECFI mailing list.
