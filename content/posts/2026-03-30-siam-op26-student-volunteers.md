The SIAM Conference on Optimization 2026 (SIAM OP26), hosted in Edinburgh, is looking for student volunteers - the more locals the better. The ask is to be a fire steward at McEwan Hall for two four-hour shifts during the conference (and to attend the mandatory training beforehand); volunteers get an "Ask me" badge to indicate that they are local, and free registration to the conference in return.

Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen via the MSBE staff list; the local organiser can be reached through him.
