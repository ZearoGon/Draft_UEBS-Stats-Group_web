The SoFiE Financial Econometrics Summer Schools are annual week-long research-based courses for PhD students and new faculty in financial econometrics. The 2026 school was held on site at the Volatility Institute of NYU Shanghai from Monday 6 to Friday 10 July 2026, with the course theme *Climate Finance*: an advanced introduction to research at the intersection of climate change, biodiversity and finance, combining theoretical frameworks, empirical methods and applied measurement approaches used to study climate and nature-related risks across asset markets, financial institutions, households and firms. Topics included climate risk pricing, stress testing and the transmission of climate risk through banks, ESG and impact investing, climate regulation and disclosure, and a dedicated module on nature and biodiversity economics and finance. Participants could present ongoing work and receive feedback from instructors and peers.

Lecturers: Professor Johannes Stroebel and Professor Theresa Kuchler, NYU Stern. Applications (CV plus a half-page motivation letter) closed on 5 May 2026. Forwarded by Heqing Shi: "The SoFiE summer school features climate finance & ESG this year."
