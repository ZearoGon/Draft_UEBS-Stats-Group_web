<h2>Key Concepts & Methodologies</h2>
  <p>Risk management relies on rigorous statistical methods to quantify uncertainty and potential losses. The field encompasses both parametric and non-parametric approaches to risk measurement.</p>

  <h3>Core Areas</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Value at Risk (VaR)</strong> - Parametric, historical simulation, and Monte Carlo approaches</li>
    <li><strong>Expected Shortfall (CVaR)</strong> - Coherent risk measures beyond VaR</li>
    <li><strong>Volatility Modelling</strong> - GARCH family models for conditional heteroskedasticity</li>
    <li><strong>Extreme Value Theory</strong> - Modelling tail risk and rare events</li>
    <li><strong>Copula Models</strong> - Dependency structures for joint risk modelling</li>
    <li><strong>Stress Testing & Scenario Analysis</strong> - Quantifying portfolio risk under extreme conditions</li>
  </ul>
