A talk in the Edinburgh SDS Hub seminar series, forwarded to the group by Dr Zexun Chen. Yusuf Mucahit Çetinkaya from Middle East Technical University (METU), Ankara, presented *Bridging AI and Personalization: From Social Media Insights to Targeted Marketing* - scalable AI-driven methods to extract meaningful insights from social media content and transform them into actionable strategies for targeted marketing, starting from Twitter account classification.

Wednesday 19 February 2025, 15:10-16:30 GMT, hybrid: Zoom and streaming at the Informatics Forum, Room 5.02.
