Following the discussion in the last session, presented by Yifan, we were curious about margins as estimates, which are in fact commonly used in experimental studies. The most basic form of testing is comparing the means of two groups, and the idea of this session is that any test is just a comparison of means. We will discuss experiments (randomised controlled trials, RCTs), quasi-experimental designs (difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity and instrumental variables), and ways of addressing selection bias (propensity score matching, synthetic control and the Heckman correction term).

For more on the methodology, Han recommends Goldfarb, A., Tucker, C., & Wang, Y. (2022). Conducting Research in Marketing with Quasi-Experiments. *Journal of Marketing*, 86(3), 1-20.
