On the afternoon of Friday 18 September 2026, Yifan Qi, Yizhuo Hu, Yuyang Jiang, Chenyang Guo and Runzhi Tian introduced the group at the Business School's PGR Welcome session.

They showed the Research Atlas on this site - the methods the group teaches and the fields they are used in - and how a session runs: in person by default, one hour from 17:00, someone starts and everyone joins in, any material and any format, and listening counts. Sessions are every other week, with dates set together; Semester 1 runs from 1 October to 10 December 2026.

What members get from it: a wider view of research in other fields, PhD-level knowledge chosen by the speakers as useful to everyone, and a close, long-term network of alumni and peers.
