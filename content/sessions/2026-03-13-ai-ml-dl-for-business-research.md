Following the consensus from the kick-off, this workshop dived into the group's most anticipated theme: AI, machine learning and deep learning. The session was a collaborative exploration around two pillars - research practice (how we are integrating AI tools into daily work) and research insights (how cutting-edge AI methodologies can improve research pipelines and inspire cross-disciplinary questions) - and closed by summarising shared interests into a knowledge-sharing agenda for future sessions.

The group was also awarded the University's Student Experience Grant at this point, which funds catering for the sessions.
