The Biometrics for Breeding Group has two postdoctoral positions open on the recently established TEAM-AI project (Tools for Evaluating and Advancing Meaningful AI Adoption in Public Breeding Programmes), a global partnership between the Roslin Institute and the UK-CGIAR Centre, including CIMMYT and National Agricultural Research Systems (NARES).

TEAM-AI aims to make advances in AI and data science deliver meaningful benefits for public breeding programmes and the small-holder farmers they serve. The roles combine expertise in (i) biometrics and quantitative genetics or (ii) machine learning and data science with real-world problems in agriculture; the resulting tools will be deployed across CGIAR and NARES programmes to support breeding decisions, crop improvement, food security and climate resilience across the Global South. Candidates should be motivated by methodological development and by turning research into practical impact.

- [Postdoctoral Research Associate in Biometrics & Quantitative Genetics for Public Breeding](https://elxw.fa.em3.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/14890)
- [Postdoctoral Research Associate in Applied Artificial Intelligence for Public Breeding](https://elxw.fa.em3.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/CX_1001/job/14891)

Informal enquiries: dtolhurs@ed.ac.uk and Dr Alexandre Hild Aono (aaono@ed.ac.uk). Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen.
