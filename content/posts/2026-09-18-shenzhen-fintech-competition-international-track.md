The International Track of the 2026 Shenzhen International FinTech Competition, run by Tsinghua PBCSF with WeBank, asks each team to design and build an AI agent that completes real tasks in a financial scenario. The theme is "AI as a Financial Participant", with six topics to choose from: a personal finance assistant, a wealth advisory agent, a cross-border and student finance assistant, an insurance assistant, an SME finance copilot, and an autonomous on-chain wallet (the track's signature topic, with a 0-5 point challenge bonus). Entries are scored on task completion (40%), security and compliance (30%), and innovation and interaction (30%).

- **Who may enter:** full-time students (undergraduate, master's or doctoral) at universities and research institutions outside Mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Macao SAR and Chinese Taipei, in teams of 2-5; cross-year, cross-major and cross-university teams are welcome. At least 60% of a team's members should be non-Chinese nationals. English throughout.
- **Deadline:** registration and submission share one deadline, 20 October 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (17:00 London). The submission includes technical documentation, a presentation deck of up to 10 minutes, source code with a GitHub repository, a security self-assessment and execution evidence; a demo video is optional.
- **Timeline:** online review from 20 October; finalists announced on 31 October; WeBank headquarters visit on 20 November; Grand Final and awards ceremony in Shenzhen on 21-23 November. At least one member of each finalist team must attend in person.
- **Fees and prizes:** no registration fee; accommodation and international travel for invited finalists are covered. Prize pool RMB 250,000 - first prize RMB 100,000, second RMB 80,000, third RMB 50,000, Best Innovation RMB 20,000, plus Excellence Awards - and fast-track interviews at WeBank for outstanding teams.

The registration form leaves the submission mailbox to be announced; check the competition website, or write to wangyr@pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn. Shared by Heqing Shi.
