Two opportunities forwarded from the Science for Sustainability (S4S) network by Dr Zexun Chen.

1. An internship opportunity at Croda (see the linked job advert).

2. The Edinburgh Earth Initiative (EEI) is accepting proposals for Earth Fellows research projects for the 2026/27 academic year: research projects related to climate and environment suitable for a postgraduate or PhD student to work on for 9 hours a week over 9 months. EEI covers the employment costs of the student for successful projects; projects must include academics from two or more Colleges, and EEI helps develop the project brief and recruit, onboard and line-manage the student fellow.
