Gaussian Processes (GPs) and their connection to Bayesian modelling. The session starts from the basics of multivariate Gaussian distributions and gradually builds up to Gaussian Processes as a prior over functions, covering posterior inference, marginal likelihood and their interpretation, and briefly exploring how GPs are used in Bayesian optimisation.
