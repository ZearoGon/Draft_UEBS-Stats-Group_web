Before you startWhat a GitHub handle is

A GitHub handle is the username of a GitHub account: the part after github.com/ in a profile address. For https://github.com/ZearoGon the handle is ZearoGon. It is not an email address and it never starts with @ here (GitHub adds the @ when it mentions someone). Letters, digits and single hyphens only, up to 39 characters, case does not matter.

To find yours: sign in to github.com, open the menu in the top-right corner - the name in bold under the avatar is the handle. Maintainers who do not have an account yet create one at github.com/signup, ideally with their university email, and add the other university alias under Settings → Emails so replies from either address count.

Why it matters: the handle is what GitHub uses to send the review email for each submission and to recognise the /approve reply. A person without a handle can hold an area on paper, but cannot approve anything until the handle is filled in below.
