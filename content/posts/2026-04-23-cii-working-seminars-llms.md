A session on large language models at the Centre for Investing Innovation (Edinburgh Futures Institute) featuring two complementary perspectives, pairing methodological innovation with lessons from practice.

*Adaptive Tokenisation and Memory in Foundation Models* - Edoardo Ponti, Assistant Professor in Natural Language Processing, School of Informatics. Tuesday 28 April 2026, 10:00-11:00, Room 4.30 EFI or online. The talk targets a fundamental inefficiency in dominant foundation-model architectures such as Transformers, whose scaling brings high energy demand and carbon emissions.

*ELM - Edinburgh University's LLM Experience* - insights from the EDINA unit. Tuesday 28 April 2026, 11:00-12:00.

Forwarded by Dr Zexun Chen via the MSBE staff list.
